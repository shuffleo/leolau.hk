# You are a plastic bag waltzing in the wind, contemplating your recurrent escapes from this landfill

Year: 2022  
Type: video, 39:43 min

---

This work is a stop-motion video animated with a series of AI-generated images. It's accompanied with computationally generative music from Alex Bainter's generative.fm project.

Multiple generations of plastic bags appear to float mid-air, journeying through landfills and painterly clouds before returning to where they started. They waltz as if choosing their own path, but the wind was always deciding. 

The title borrows from the YouTube mixtapes that kept people company during COVID lockdown: long, ambient, titled like gentle instructions for getting through the day.

The video was made the day before the artist turned 30, when Hong Kong is experiencing one of the greatest political unravellings, and when the world is navigating the technical marvels of GenAI.

The bag keeps waltzing. It doesn't get out.
